About InProgress — a simple online to-do list

Focus on five tasks, keep everything else in sight

InProgress is a free online task planner for everyday work, study, and personal projects. Capture what you want to do in your Open list, then commit to up to five tasks in your Closed list. Finish a task or return it to Open before taking on another.

Plan deadlines with a 30-day calendar

Choose a date to set a deadline, see how many days remain, and hover over calendar dates to view the tasks due. Task colors help you spot approaching and overdue deadlines.

Keep your task list simple

Drag tasks to reorder them within their priority group, double-click to edit a title, and use Undo after finishing or removing a task. Switch between light and dark themes to suit your workspace.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for guest mode. Guest tasks stay in this browser. Sign in with Google to save your account tasks privately with Firebase and sync them across devices. You choose whether to add existing browser tasks to your account.
